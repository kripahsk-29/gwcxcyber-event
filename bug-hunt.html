🐛 Bug Hunt 🎃

A Halloween code-debugging game — Escape the Bug

You'll see 8 short spooky programs written in plain-English pseudocode. Each one is SUPPOSED to do something simple — but a bug snuck in! Your team clicks the line you think is broken, then picks the fix.

  • ✅ +10 points for clicking the buggy line on your first try (+5 on your second try)
  • ✅ +10 points for picking the correct fix
  • 💡 −3 points if you use the one hint allowed per level
  • 🔍 −3 points if you use "Trace It" to step through the code's values
  • ⏭️ Stuck? Hit Skip to move on without losing the whole game
  • ⏳ You have 10 minutes for all 8 levels — the timer turns orange, then red!
